# NSDI 2024 论文列表

NSDI 2024 正式技术议程共收录 112 篇论文。以下标题链接至 USENIX 官方页面；每篇依次列出作者与机构、研究场景和系统机制、相较既有方法的核心创新，以及细粒度方向。112 份官方 PDF 均已保存至 `reference/NSDI/2024/`。

- [Horus: Granular In-Network Task Scheduler for Cloud Datacenters](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/yassini)
  - Parham Yassini, Simon Fraser University; Khaled Diab, Hewlett Packard Labs; Saeed Zangeneh and Mohamed Hefeeda, Simon Fraser University
  - Horus 面向数据中心中需在微秒级分派的短任务，让多台可编程交换机分布式维护 worker 负载状态并在线速上并行选 worker；调度策略限制状态传播，不在交换机内缓存任务，可扩展至 2.7 万台主机的全数据中心。
  - 相较只能在单 rack 内工作的 in-network scheduler，核心创新是把 worker state 压缩、分发和调度都做成可横跨多级交换网的分布式协议。
  - In-network Scheduling、短任务、可编程交换机、Worker State、尾延迟

- [Fast Vector Query Processing for Large Datasets Beyond GPU Memory with Reordered Pipelining](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/zhang-zili-pipelining)
  - Zili Zhang, Fangyue Liu, Gang Huang, Xuanzhe Liu, and Xin Jin, School of Computer Science, Peking University
  - RUMMY 在十亿级向量数据超过 GPU 显存时，将 host-to-GPU 传输与向量检索 pipeline 起来：先按 cluster 合并 batch 中重复数据，再用动态 kernel padding/均衡和 query-aware 重排同时填满 GPU 与 PCIe，并定制显存分配以减少碎片和 cache miss。
  - 相较 CUDA unified memory 按页被动换入，核心创新是利用向量查询间的 cluster 共享关系重排工作，从而主动消除传输重复并隐藏超显存访问。
  - Vector Database、GPU Query Processing、Out-of-core、Reordered Pipeline、PCIe 重叠

- [LoLKV: The Logless, Linearizable, RDMA-based Key-Value Storage System](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/alquraan)
  - Ahmed Alquraan and Sreeharsha Udayashankar, University of Waterloo; Virendra Marathe, Oracle Labs; Bernard Wong and Samer Al-Kiswany, University of Waterloo
  - LoLKV 是面向低延迟内存 KV 的线性一致复制系统：它不用传统 replicated log，而让多线程以 lock-free 方式直接更新对象，follower 保持被动，故障时再通过新的 leader election 和 consolidation 恢复唯一状态。
  - 相较所有更新先排入日志的 KV 库，核心创新是用对象级并发更新和故障后 consolidation 取代正常路径的全局日志序列。
  - Distributed KV、Linearizability、RDMA、Logless Replication、Lock-free

- [Making Kernel Bypass Practical for the Cloud with Junction](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/fried)
  - Joshua Fried and Gohar Irfan Chaudhry, MIT CSAIL; Enrique Saurez, Esha Choukse, and Íñigo Goiri, Azure Research – Systems; Sameh Elnikety, Microsoft Research; Rodrigo Fonseca, Azure Research – Systems; Adam Belay, MIT CSAIL
  - Junction 让未修改的 Linux 网络应用在云上使用 kernel bypass：它利用新 NIC 特性减少每实例 pinned memory 和大量 queue 的 polling 成本，并让 userspace network stack 与应用共享地址空间以低成本兼容 syscall，单机可密集放置数千实例。
  - 相较需要独占核、大量固定内存和重写应用的 kernel-bypass runtime，核心创新是同时解决高密度资源复用和 Linux binary compatibility。
  - Kernel Bypass、Cloud Runtime、Linux Compatibility、NIC Queue、高密度多租户

- [Sifter: An Inversion-Free and Large-Capacity Programmable Packet Scheduler](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/gao-peixuan)
  - Peixuan Gao, Anthony Dalleggio, Jiajin Liu, and Chen Peng, New York University; Yang Xu, Fudan University; H. Jonathan Chao, New York University
  - Sifter 为大流数交换机做可编程优先级排队：它用 Sift Sorting 把大 rank 粗分到 bucket，只将当前小 rank 放入小型 PIFO 精排，并利用 memory/output bandwidth 的 speed-up factor 保证在 100 Gbps 下不发生 packet inversion。
  - 相较容量小但精确的 PIFO 和容量大但可乱序的近似队列，核心创新是粗粒度 bucket 与小 PIFO 并行的可证无逆序硬件排序。
  - Packet Scheduling、PIFO、FPGA、Inversion-free、100Gbps

- [Flow Scheduling with Imprecise Knowledge](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/li-wenxin)
  - Wenxin Li, Xin He, Yuan Liu, and Keqiu Li, Tianjin University; Kai Chen, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and University of Science and Technology of China; Zhao Ge and Zewei Guan, Tianjin University; Heng Qi, Dalian University of Technology; Song Zhang, Tianjin University; Guyue Liu, New York University Shanghai
  - QCLIMB 在无法精确知道每条数据中心 flow size 时，用机器学习估计大小上下界，调度器根据 lower bound 从传输初始就优先可能的小流，并在终端修复因优先级变化导致的乱序。
  - 相较要求精确 flow size 的 pFabric 或完全不用先验的 PIAS，核心创新是将“不精确但有界”的信息直接转换为可实现的流优先级。
  - Datacenter Flow Scheduling、Flow-size Bounds、ML for Networks、FCT、Out-of-order

- [Pudica: Toward Near-Zero Queuing Delay in Congestion Control for Cloud Gaming](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/wang-shibo)
  - Shibo Wang, Xi’an Jiaotong University and Tencent Inc.; Shusen Yang, Xi'an Jiaotong University; Xiao Kong, Chenglei Wu, and Longwei Jiang, Tencent; Chenren Xu, Peking University; Cong Zhao, Xi'an Jiaotong University; Xuesong Yang, Bonree; Jianjun Xiao and Xin Liu, Tencent; Changxi Zheng, Pixel Lab, Tencent America, and Columbia University; Jing Wang and Honghao Liu, Tencent
  - Pudica 面向云游戏帧传输，不直接追测绝对带宽，而通过 paced frame 探测 bandwidth-utilization ratio，再联合调整编码 bitrate，使链路保持高利用的同时几乎不建队、不破坏跨流公平性；已服务数百万玩家。
  - 相较以 bandwidth estimate 直接推高发送率的视频 CC，核心创新是用利用率而非容量作为控制信号，针对帧级 burst 抑制自诱导排队。
  - Cloud Gaming、Congestion Control、Near-zero Queue、Bitrate Control、生产部署

- [Revisiting Congestion Control for Lossless Ethernet](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/zhang-yiran)
  - Yiran Zhang, Tsinghua University and Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications; Qingkai Meng, Tsinghua University and Beihang University; Chaolei Hu and Fengyuan Ren, Tsinghua University
  - ACC 利用 lossless Ethernet 的 packet conservation 特性，仅从 ACK time series 估计 pipe capacity 和多余在途 packet；它精确暂停拥塞流以排空多余包，然后将速率匹配容量，并分别处理 congestion flow 与受 HoL blocking 的 victim flow。
  - 相较通用 loss/ECN 反应式 CC，核心创新是把无损网络的守恒结构变成可计算的排队与容量信号，从而主动消除 congestion spreading。
  - Lossless Ethernet、Congestion Control、ACK Time Series、HoL Blocking、Deadlock

- [Autothrottle: A Practical Bi-Level Approach to Resource Management for SLO-Targeted Microservices](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/wang-zibo)
  - Zibo Wang, University of Science and Technology of China and Microsoft Research; Pinghe Li, ETH Zurich; Chieh-Jan Mike Liang, Microsoft Research; Feng Wu, University of Science and Technology of China; Francis Y. Yan, Microsoft Research Awarded Outstanding Paper!
  - Autothrottle 为有端到端 latency SLO 的 microservice graph 分两层控制 CPU：慢速的应用级 learning controller 根据整条 request path 设定各服务的 CPU throttle-ratio target，快速的服务级 heuristic controller 在本地跟踪目标，以应对各层时间尺度不同。
  - 相较一个 controller 直接从端到端延迟调每个 container，核心创新是以 performance target 作为两层控制器的稳定接口，分离全局 SLO 学习与局部资源执行。
  - Microservices、CPU Throttling、Latency SLO、Bi-level Control、Resource Management

- [Jolteon: Unleashing the Promise of Serverless for Serverless Workflows](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/zhang-zili-jolteon)
  - Zili Zhang, Chao Jin, and Xin Jin, School of Computer Science, Peking University
  - Jolteon 为多阶段 serverless workflow 自动选择每个 function 的资源配置：白盒模型表达 serverless 执行结构，黑盒随机部分拟合云性能波动，然后求解 chance-constrained optimization，在指定成本或延迟置信边界下配资源。
  - 相较将 workflow 每个阶段独立 profiling 或使用确定性模型，核心创新是用混合随机性能模型对端到端约束给出概率保证。
  - Serverless Workflow、Resource Provisioning、Chance Constraint、Cost-Latency、AWS Lambda

- [Can't Be Late: Optimizing Spot Instance Savings under Deadlines](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/wu-zhanghao)
  - Zhanghao Wu, Wei-Lin Chiang, Ziming Mao, and Zongheng Yang, University of California, Berkeley; Eric Friedman and Scott Shenker, University of California, Berkeley, and ICSI; Ion Stoica, University of California, Berkeley Awarded Outstanding Paper!
  - 该工作研究 deadline job 如何同时使用便宜但可被抢占的 spot VM 和保底 on-demand VM：Uniform Progress 政策不假设 spot availability 分布，按截止时间前应有的均匀进度决定何时切换保底资源，并在 SkyPilot 上验证多云 workload。
  - 相较“只要 spot 可用就一直用”的 greedy policy，核心创新是无参数、无可用性预测依赖的进度不变量，同时给出 worst/average-case 分析。
  - Spot Instance、Deadline Scheduling、Cloud Cost、Online Policy、SkyPilot

- [Towards Intelligent Automobile Cockpit via A New Container Architecture](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/jiang-lin)
  - Lin Jiang and Feiyu Zhang, Xi’an Yunzhiji Technology; Jiang Ming, Tulane University
  - AutoVP 面向智能汽车座舱，将安全/实时任务放到车规 MCU，将娱乐和数字仪表等多媒体任务整合到消费级多核 SoC 上的多个 Android container，用两芯片边界保持关键性隔离并降低对短缺 microhypervisor 芯片的依赖。
  - 相较把所有座舱功能都放在 microhypervisor 虚拟机中，核心创新是按关键性分拆硬件，再对大多数非安全任务使用更低成本的 container consolidation。
  - Automotive System、Container、Mixed Criticality、Android、SoC-MCU

- [MuCache: A General Framework for Caching in Microservice Graphs](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/zhang-haoran)
  - Haoran Zhang, Konstantinos Kallas, and Spyros Pavlatos, Rajeev Alur, Sebastian Angel, and Vincent Liu, University of Pennsylvania
  - MuCache 可在任意 microservice graph 的服务边上加 cache：当下游状态未变时直接绕过 RPC，状态改变时通过面向 graph topology 的非阻塞 coherence/invalidation protocol 传播；论文证明加缓存后的每个可观测执行都等价于原应用的某个执行。
  - 相较在单个服务内手工加 cache，核心创新是能跨服务图自动复用结果、又有强语义保证的非阻塞失效协议。
  - Microservice Cache、Cache Coherence、Graph Topology、Non-blocking、Correctness

- [A large-scale deployment of DCTCP](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/dhamija)
  - Abhishek Dhamija and Balasubramanian Madhavan, Meta; Hechao Li, Netflix; Jie Meng, Shrikrishna Khare, and Madhavi Rao, Meta; Lawrence Brakmo; Neil Spring, Prashanth Kannan, and Srikanth Sundaresan, Meta; Soudeh Ghorbani, Meta and Johns Hopkins University
  - 该经验报告复盘 DCTCP 在大规模生产数据中心的推广：它记录 ECN/队列阈值部署后丢包改善，也详细分析 DCTCP 与其他流量类的公平性、主机/交换机实现 bug 和分阶段 rollout 障碍。
  - 相较只在测试床评估 DCTCP 的论文，核心价值是揭示“算法已成熟”之后仍会决定生产成败的异构流量共存、设备语义和操作问题。
  - DCTCP、Production Deployment、ECN、Fairness、Operational Experience

- [TECC: Towards Efficient QUIC Tunneling via Collaborative Transmission Control](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/zhang-jiaxing)
  - Jiaxing Zhang, Alibaba Group, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences; Furong Yang, Alibaba Group; Ting Liu, Alibaba Group, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences; Qinghua Wu, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Purple Mountain Laboratories, China; Wu Zhao, Yuanbo Zhang, Wentao Chen, Yanmei Liu, Hongyu Guo, and Yunfei Ma, Alibaba Group; Zhenyu Li, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Purple Mountain Laboratories, China
  - TECC 解决 QUIC tunnel 中内层 end-to-end QUIC 和外层 tunnel connection 各自拥塞控制、导致发送节奏不匹配的问题；隧道服务器把其观测到的精细网络状态反馈给端主机，让内层连接协同调整 CC 和 loss recovery。
  - 相较内外两个 transport loop 独立运行的 QUIC tunneling，核心创新是建立跨隧道层的显式 feedback channel，消除两层对网络的相互误判。
  - QUIC Tunnel、Congestion Control、Loss Recovery、Cross-layer Feedback、RPC Tail Latency

- [iStack: A General and Stateful Name-based Protocol Stack for Named Data Networking](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/li-tianlong)
  - Tianlong Li, Tian Song, and Yating Yang, Beijing Institute of Technology
  - iStack 把 Named Data Networking 做成通用 OS kernel protocol stack：内核 name-resolution 同时支持网络转发与本地 demultiplexing，two-layer face 将 name-based path 映射到 MAC/IP/port interface，socket-compatible API 使应用可按现有方式调用，紧凑表结构则控制 per-packet state 开销。
  - 相较独立 userspace NDN forwarder，核心创新是在现有地址定位内核中加入从命名、socket 到 per-packet cache/state 的完整协议栈抽象。
  - Named Data Networking、Kernel Network Stack、Name Resolution、Socket API、In-kernel Cache

- [Cloudcast: High-Throughput, Cost-Aware Overlay Multicast in the Cloud](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/wooders)
  - Sarah Wooders and Shu Liu, UC Berkeley; Paras Jain, Genmo AI; Xiangxi Mo and Joseph Gonzalez, UC Berkeley; Vincent Liu, University of Pennsylvania; Ion Stoica, UC Berkeley
  - Cloudcast 为跨 region/跨 provider 的大宗数据复制构造 overlay multicast tree：optimizer 联合云链路吞吐、egress 计价和用户 runtime budget，可临时启动 waypoint 中继数据，选择在时限内费用最低的复制树。
  - 相较各目标 region 独立从 source 拉取或固定单云 multicast，核心创新是将可弹性 waypoint 和云价格函数一起放入有完成时限的树优化。
  - Multi-cloud、Overlay Multicast、Egress Cost、Bulk Replication、Waypoint

- [Understanding Routable PCIe Performance for Composable Infrastructures](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/hou)
  - Wentao Hou, University of Wisconsin-Madison; Jie Zhang and Zeke Wang, Zhejiang University; Ming Liu, University of Wisconsin-Madison
  - rPCIeBench 是用于 composable infrastructure 的路由化 PCIe 软硬协同 benchmark：它提供可编排的 PCIe 传输 primitive 和端到端 transaction observability，量化跨多个 non-transparent bridge/switching domain 的延迟、带宽同步和干扰，再将规律编成 shared-fabric flow 性能预测器。
  - 相较把远端 PCIe device 当作本地设备、只测单链路，核心创新是暴露多 switching domain 内的 transaction/traffic orchestration，并提炼为可指导 scheduler 的约束。
  - Composable Infrastructure、Routable PCIe、Benchmark、Fabric Contention、Performance Model

- [Alea-BFT: Practical Asynchronous Byzantine Fault Tolerance](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/antunes)
  - Diogo S. Antunes, Afonso N. Oliveira, André Breda, Matheus Guilherme Franco, Henrique Moniz, and Rodrigo Rodrigues, Instituto Superior Técnico (ULisboa) and INESC-ID
  - Alea-BFT 是不假设消息延迟上界的 asynchronous BFT：每个实例先由指定 replica 做高效 reliable broadcast，再用低成本 binary agreement 决定提案，两阶段 pipeline 让领导者集中正常路径工作，又不依赖 timeout，已用于 Ethereum distributed validator。
  - 相较构造复杂随机化子协议的 asynchronous BFT，核心创新是把经典 leader-driven 快路径重新组合进 broadcast + binary agreement，同时获得易实现性和延迟优势。
  - Asynchronous BFT、State Machine Replication、Reliable Broadcast、Binary Agreement、Blockchain

- [Harmony: A Congestion-free Datacenter Architecture](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/agarwal-saksham)
  - Saksham Agarwal, Qizhe Cai, Rachit Agarwal, and David Shmoys, Cornell University; Amin Vahdat, Google
  - Harmony 为数据中心网络提供 congestion-free message delivery：发送端只有在一条路径已预留可控服务时才注入消息，因此无故障时每一跳排队有界、不会因 buffer overflow 丢包，同时保持接近最优链路利用率。
  - 相较先过量发包再由 CC 收敛的 best-effort fabric，核心创新是以极小附加延迟为主机暴露可证的 per-switch queue bound 和无拥塞丢包语义。
  - Datacenter Network、Congestion-free、Bounded Queuing、Lossless Delivery、Network Architecture

- [SwiftPaxos: Fast Geo-Replicated State Machines](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/ryabinin)
  - Fedor Ryabinin, IMDEA Software Institute and Universidad Politécnica de Madrid; Alexey Gotsman, IMDEA Software Institute; Pierre Sutra, Télécom SudParis and INRIA
  - SwiftPaxos 为 geo-replicated state machine 在低冲突时以 2 个 message delay 提交，有冲突时以 3 个 delay 完成；replica 先为自己观察到的 command order 投票，若分歧可第二次跟随 leader，无需整轮重新投票。
  - 相较低冲突快、但冲突时会退化到比 Paxos 更慢的 fast-path protocol，核心创新是允许每个 replica 两阶段投票，在不牺牲 worst case 的前提下降低 best case。
  - Geo-replication、Paxos、State Machine Replication、Fast Path、Contention

- [The Bedrock of Byzantine Fault Tolerance: A Unified Platform for BFT Protocols Analysis, Implementation, and Experimentation](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/amiri)
  - Mohammad Javad Amiri, Stony Brook University; Chenyuan Wu, University of Pennsylvania; Divyakant Agrawal and Amr El Abbadi, UC Santa Barbara; Boon Thau Loo, University of Pennsylvania; Mohammad Sadoghi, UC Davis Awarded Outstanding Paper!
  - Bedrock 把 BFT 的 communication topology、commit strategy、order fairness 等差异拆成统一 design-space dimension，研究者可从组件组合出多种现有/新协议，并在同一 implementation/deployment harness 下比较性能与语义取舍。
  - 相较每篇 BFT 论文重写一套不可直接比较的系统，核心创新是将协议族的共性参数化，使分析、实现和实验共用一个可组合平台。
  - BFT、Protocol Design Space、Experiment Platform、Order Fairness、Composable Protocol

- [DINT: Fast In-Kernel Distributed Transactions with eBPF](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/zhou-yang)
  - Yang Zhou, Harvard University; Xingyu Xiang, Peking University; Matthew Kiley, Harvard University; Sowmya Dharanipragada, Cornell University; Minlan Yu, Harvard University
  - DINT 在保留 Linux kernel network stack 安全、隔离和可调试性的前提下做可串行化分布式内存事务；它用 eBPF 把频繁的 transaction fast-path operation 下沉到内核，避免多次 socket/userspace 路径，却不需改内核或写自定义 module。
  - 相较为性能绕过内核的 RDMA/DPDK transaction system，核心创新是把事务语义直接编入内核原生 eBPF fast path，在不放弃 OS protection 时达到 kernel-bypass 级性能。
  - Distributed Transaction、eBPF、Kernel Network Stack、Serializability、In-memory

- [Brain-on-Switch: Towards Advanced Intelligent Network Data Plane via NN-Driven Traffic Analysis at Line-Speed](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/yan)
  - Jinzhu Yan and Haotian Xu, Tsinghua University; Zhuotao Liu, Qi Li, Ke Xu, Mingwei Xu, and Jianping Wu, Tsinghua University and Zhongguancun Laboratory
  - BoS 在 P4 switch 线速执行面向序列流量的 RNN：定制 data-plane-friendly recurrent architecture 用有限 pipeline stage 支持无限 time step，原始 packet sequence 不需手工 feature；交换机外的 Transformer 再对难例或长程依赖补充分析。
  - 相较只能在 match-action table 中实现 tree/forest 且受限于可计算 feature 的 INDP，核心创新是将 recurrent inference 映射为可跨包保留状态的有界流水线。
  - In-network ML、P4、RNN、Traffic Analysis、Line-rate Inference

- [The Eternal Tussle: Exploring the Role of Centralization in IPFS](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/wei)
  - Yiluo Wei, Hong Kong University of Science & Technology (GZ); Dennis Trautwein and Yiannis Psaras, Protocol Labs; Ignacio Castro, Queen Mary University of London; Will Scott, Protocol Labs; Aravindh Raman, Brave Software; Gareth Tyson, Hong Kong University of Science & Technology (GZ)
  - 该工作从 IPFS 维护者角度评估三个“中心化补丁”：InterPlanetary Network Indexer 集中查内容位置，Hydra Booster 作为战略性 DHT 节点加速路由，HTTP Gateway 提供公共入口；实测这些组件如何用部分中心化换取可用性、搜索和性能。
  - 相较将去中心化视为二元目标，核心贡献是给出大规模 IPFS 中可操作的混合架构和真实部署证据，揭示哪些 control/discovery function 最需集中化。
  - IPFS、Decentralized Web、DHT、Content Index、Hybrid Architecture

- [BBQ: A Fast and Scalable Integer Priority Queue for Hardware Packet Scheduling](https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi24/presentation/atre)
